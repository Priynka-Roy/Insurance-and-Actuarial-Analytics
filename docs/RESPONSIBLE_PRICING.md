# Responsible pricing and supported use

ActuaryLab AI is an educational actuarial analytics project. It supports explanation of exposure-aware frequency, positive-payment severity, pure premium, retrospective model validation and hypothetical portfolio risk. It is not a live underwriting service, production quote engine, reserving system, Solvency II internal model or regulator-approved tariff.

Driver age, location, density and bonus-malus can describe predictive associations and may act as proxies for other personal or socioeconomic attributes. Predictive performance does not establish legal admissibility, causal effect, ethical acceptability, or pricing fairness. The project does not claim Finnish or EU regulatory compliance and does not interpret a calibration difference as automatic evidence of unlawful discrimination.

Group diagnostics show group size and exposure alongside observed and expected outcomes. Sparse claim counts and large losses can make ratios unstable; uncertainty and sparse flags must accompany interpretation. A model can calibrate in aggregate while failing within a subgroup. Small cells cannot support strong individual conclusions.

All prices, expense shares, risk loadings and margins in the app are hypothetical user assumptions. Actual commercial earned premiums and expenses are absent from the source. Modeled and simulated loss ratios therefore do not measure any insurer's profitability. Synthetic examples are illustrations rather than offers, eligibility decisions, or advice to an actual policyholder.

An operational project would require verified rights to relevant contemporary data, lawful-variable review, external actuarial validation, prospective monitoring, coverage and inflation adjustments, documented customer impact assessment, access controls and an insurer's governance process. This educational repository does not provide that operational approval. It has no employer, insurer sponsor or endorsement and does not claim professional actuarial certification.
