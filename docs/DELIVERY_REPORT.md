# ActuaryLab AI — final delivery report

Completed 16 October 2026. Source retrieval/license review: 15 October 2026.

1. **Executive summary:** A complete historical insurance analytics project: audited real motor policy/payment data, exposure-aware GLMs and ML comparison, actuarial validation, pricing scenarios and aggregate process-risk simulation. Existing projects and the personal portfolio were not modified.
2. **GitHub:** https://github.com/Priynka-Roy/actuarylab-ai-insurance-pricing-risk — public, main branch, Priynka Roy commit attribution. Analytical initial revision: 2dac266f0f607626166137f78d27505dc202791f.
3. **Data provenance/license:** Official OpenML datasets 41214 and 41215, version 1, original ARFF files verified against official MD5 plus recorded SHA-256. OpenML declares CC0; upstream CASdatasets package GPL licensing is separately disclosed. Raw records remain local and excluded. [Data card](DATA_CARD.md).
4. **Actual source size:** 678,013 unique policy rows and 26,639 payment rows. The upstream narrative 677,991 is preserved as a documented discrepancy.
5. **Reconciliation:** 195 unmatched payment rows, 255 duplicate-looking payment rows retained, and 9,117 policy count disagreements (9,116 with declared claims and absent payments). Strict economic cohort: 668,896 policies. No silent zero-filling, count repair, payment clipping or exposure capping. [Policy and audit](DATA_RECONCILIATION.md).
6. **Exposure:** Source total 358,499.445462984 years; reconciled total 353,893.168031 years. Retained 1,224 records above one year; no nonpositive source exposures. Primary frequency 0.07472029 positive payments/exposure-year is different from claiming-policy proportion 3.7290%.
7. **Frequency:** Intercept, unpenalized and L2 Poisson GLMs, categorical Poisson HGB, and development NB2 diagnostic. Training-only encoders/scalers; annual count/exposure response with exposure weights. Numerical count-offset/deviance equivalence tested.
8. **Severity:** Intercept and Gamma log GLMs on individual strictly positive payments, inheriting policy split. Primary payment values stay uncapped; training-p99 cap refit is a separate development diagnostic.
9. **Tweedie:** Six direct candidates use fitted powers 1.3/1.5/1.7 and alpha 0.001/0.1. All premium comparisons use the same exposure-weighted evaluation power 1.5; direct components are not falsely inferred from Tweedie.
10. **ML comparison:** Frequency HGB with 15 leaves scores 0.449373 holdout Poisson deviance/exposure versus 0.461383 for the unpenalized GLM. Interpretability and distribution-fit limitations remain explicit.
11. **Selection:** Frozen seed 42 policy hash split: 401,433 train  / 133,582 validation  / 133,881 test. Selection is validation-only, before test. Chosen: Poisson HGB leaves=15; Gamma GLM alpha=0.1; Two-part frequency × severity. No holdout-based reselection; the best holdout Gamma candidate is deliberately not substituted.
12. **Actual scores:** Selected Gamma holdout deviance 2.025686, O/E 1.2039. Two-part common-power deviance 80.463183; best development-selected direct Tweedie 86.392864. Full candidate tables remain in [results](RESULTS.md).
13. **Uncertainty:** 250 ordinary policy-cluster bootstrap resamples, paired for comparisons, pointwise 95% percentile intervals conditional on fixed models. Frequency difference HGB−GLM: −0.012010 [−0.014954,−0.009364]. Direct−two-part premium difference:+5.929681 [0.519298,16.444397]. No training/selection or temporal-shift coverage claimed.
14. **Pure premium:** Primary observed 169.2846 SMU/year. Holdout predicted loss 11,389,868.69 versus observed 13,477,614.76 SMU: 15.49% underprediction, O/E 1.1833. Holdout observed pure-premium bootstrap interval 125.10–316.69 SMU/year; selected premium O/E interval 0.7809–1.9784. Calibration weakness is reported, not repaired using test outcomes.
15. **Ranking:** Exposure-based risk deciles and concentration curves; holdout exposure concentration index 0.51403. Defined as 1−2 times area under cumulative loss-share versus exposure-share, ordered by increasing selected annual premium. It is not normalized Gini, accuracy, AUC, causal discrimination or a fairness certificate.
16. **Pricing:** Synthetic profiles and coherent expense/profit shares of final premium. Risk loads pure premium; contract-period cost and premium multiply by exposure. Default hypothetical expense 20%, profit 5%, risk 5%, fixed 0. Fixed premium under combined+20% frequency/severity stress raises expected loss ratio71.43% → 102.86%; no measured insurer profitability.
17. **Monte Carlo:**1,000 synthetic one-year policies, eight fitted profiles equally weighted, 10,000 simulated portfolios per saved scenario, seed 442. Batched Poisson counts plus conditional Gamma/lognormal severity; no giant claims-by-policy tensor. Bounded cached custom simulation runs only on submit. [Construction](RISK_SIMULATION.md).
18. **Expected aggregate loss:** Baseline analytical 262,754.85 SMU; MC mean 263,178.44, mean SE 1,346.05 (difference 0.315 SE). All seven scenario mean discrepancies are below 4 estimated meanSE; no seeds were selected to conceal sampling error.
19. **VaR/ES:** Baseline VaR95 512,611.75, ES95 611,360.58; VaR99 663,142.45, ES99 761,228.67 SMU. Empirical inverse-CDF VaR and exact fractional upper-tail mass ES handle ties. These are not regulatory capital requirements.
20. **Tail sensitivity:** Same mean under lognormal assumptions gives ES95 367,810.71 versus Gamma 611,360.58. A mean-one common-frequency shock (assumed lognormal sigma 0.35) preserves expected loss and gives ES95 702,798.17. Only 100 simulated portfolios occupy the 99% tail. Tail specifications are assumptions, not a validated catastrophe/reinsurance estimate.
21. **Responsible pricing:** Driver age/geography/proxy concerns, subgroup counts/exposure, sparse-group flags and calibration are visible. Associations do not establish causal effects, fairness, lawful rating-factor use or Finnish/EU compliance. [Responsible use](RESPONSIBLE_PRICING.md).
